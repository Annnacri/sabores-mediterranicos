# 🍽️ Sabores Mediterrânicos

**Catering website for private events and small celebrations in Lisbon, Portugal.**

🌐 **Live website:** https://sabores-mediterranicos.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Overview

**Sabores Mediterrânicos** is a responsive commercial website created for a Lisbon-based catering service serving private events, birthdays, brunches, family gatherings and small corporate events.

The website was designed with a strong focus on:

* Clear service presentation
* Food and menu discovery
* Event-specific use cases
* Lead generation
* Quote requests
* Mobile-friendly browsing
* Simple conversion flow

The primary business objective is to turn website visitors into qualified catering enquiries.

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 🏠 Landing Page

The homepage communicates the service proposition immediately, including:

* Catering service in Lisbon
* Minimum order of 10 people
* Capacity of approximately 10–50 guests
* Main catering specialties
* Event types
* Service area
* Quote request CTA

### 🍴 Food & Menu Showcase

The website presents the most requested specialties, including:

* Bacalhau à Brás
* Lombo no forno com mel e ervas aromáticas

It also presents additional catering categories:

* Portuguese petiscos
* Savoury snacks
* Cheese and charcuterie boards
* Brunch
* Finger food
* Desserts
* Portuguese pastries
* Fresh fruit

The menu structure allows customers to request a customized proposal instead of being restricted to a fixed package.

### 🎉 Event Categories

The website helps visitors identify whether the service fits their event:

* Birthdays
* Private parties
* Family and friend gatherings
* Brunches
* Special celebrations
* Small corporate events

### 📝 Catering Quote Form

A structured enquiry form collects the information required to prepare a catering proposal:

* Name
* Email
* Phone / WhatsApp
* Event type
* Event date
* Event location
* Number of guests
* Food preferences
* Additional information

Customers can also request a **custom menu suggestion**.

### 📍 Service Area

The website communicates the main delivery area around Lisbon, including nearby municipalities such as:

* Lisbon
* Oeiras
* Cascais
* Sintra
* Amadora
* Odivelas
* Loures
* Almada
* Margem Sul

Availability is confirmed according to the event location and schedule.

## 🎯 UX & Conversion Strategy

The website follows a simple conversion journey:

```text
Discover the service
        ↓
Explore food & event options
        ↓
Understand delivery area
        ↓
Request a customized menu
        ↓
Submit catering enquiry
```

Rather than forcing customers to choose a complete menu immediately, the website allows them to describe their event and request a tailored proposal.

This reduces friction for customers who know they need catering but are unsure about quantities or menu combinations.
